About the flower

Elegant, fragrant and timeless

The red rose is one of the most recognisable flowers in the world. It is admired for its rich colour, pleasant fragrance, delicate petals and thorny stems.

Roses belong to the Rosa genus. They have been cultivated for centuries and are now grown in gardens and landscapes across many countries.

Because of its striking appearance, the red rose is often given as a gift to express affection, appreciation and deep love.

What it represents

❤️

Red roses are widely associated with romantic love, devotion and heartfelt affection.

✨

Beauty

🤝

A red rose can also communicate respect, gratitude and appreciation for someone special.

Keep roses healthy

Simple rose care tips

  1. Give sunlight: Choose a position that receives several hours of sunlight each day.
  2. Water deeply: Water the soil around the roots rather than constantly wetting the leaves.
  3. Use healthy soil: Well-drained, fertile soil helps roses develop strong roots and flowers.
  4. Prune carefully: Remove dead or damaged growth to encourage healthy new growth.
  5. Watch for pests: Check leaves and stems regularly and respond early to signs of pests or disease.
